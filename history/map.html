Map

Where UC open source work started, and where to find help on each campus today. Orange pins mark where the work happened. Navy pins mark the UC OSPO Network campuses. Select a pin to open the story or the campus office. Use the box at the top right to show or hide each layer.

Some projects began outside UC, or online. Their pins mark the UC part of the story, and the “On campus today” box in each chapter names the earlier place. Pins mark public buildings or campuses only.

Where the work happened

  • ARPANET Node 1 and early RFC work. Boelter Hall, room 3420, UCLA
  • ARPANET Node 3 and the Culler–Fried Online System. North Hall, UCSB Computer Center, UC Santa Barbara
  • BSD TCP/IP stack (4.2BSD). Evans Hall — Computer Systems Research Group, UC Berkeley
  • Berkeley Software Distribution Unix. Evans Hall — Berkeley UNIX distribution and CSRG, UC Berkeley
  • INGRES and Berkeley POSTGRES. UC Berkeley — Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences / Electronics Research Laboratory, UC Berkeley
  • Time Zone Database (tz). UCLA (current coordinator), Online / UCLA today
  • Apache HTTP Server. online, Online / San Francisco connection (no pin: not tied to one place)
  • UCSC Genome Browser and public human genome assembly. UC Santa Cruz — Genome Bioinformatics Group, UC Santa Cruz
  • EEGLAB and the ICA/EEG Toolbox. Swartz Center for Computational Neuroscience, San Diego Supercomputer Center, Salk Institute / UC San Diego
  • Cytoscape. Ideker Lab, Biomedical Research Facility II, UC San Diego, Institute for Systems Biology / UC San Diego
  • IPython and Project Jupyter. UC Berkeley, University of Colorado Boulder / UC Berkeley
  • Apache Spark. Soda Hall — RAD Lab and later AMPLab, UC Berkeley
  • Ceph. UC Santa Cruz — Storage Systems Research Center, UC Santa Cruz
  • RISC-V. Soda Hall — Parallel Computing Laboratory, UC Berkeley
  • Named Data Networking. UCLA — Internet Research Laboratory, UCLA
  • Ray. Soda Hall — AMPLab–RISELab transition, UC Berkeley
