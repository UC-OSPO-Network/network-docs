# CLDT Cohort Recruitment — Communication Plan

Outreach plan for the blog post [Develop an Open Source Lesson with the UC OSPO Network](https://ucospo.net/posts/develop-open-source-lesson-cldt/). Same approach as the IMLS pilot push: one email, one social set, and a channel-by-channel posting list.

## Key facts (keep these straight across every channel)

* Four CLDT cohorts run summer/fall. The software licensing cohort is **full**. **Three are still forming.**
* **Option 1:** Join the scoped "Librarians as Open Source Stewards" cohort. Needs **2-4 people**.
* **Option 2:** Two open slots for a **new** lesson. To propose you only need a topic and a rough audience. No team required up front, the network helps recruit one.
* Prerequisite: at least one team member has completed Carpentries Instructor Training or Learner-Centered Teaching.
* Payoff: lessons enter the UC OSPO catalog and get taught; path to Carpentries Lab peer review, a JOSE (CrossRef DOI, Google Scholar) or Zenodo DOI, repo templates, and institutional continuity if you move.
* **Interest deadline: Friday, July 17** (extended from June 30; see ucospo.net PR #339).
* Respond: [GitHub interest issue](https://github.com/UC-OSPO-Network/education/issues/new?template=cldt-interest.yml) or email tdennis@library.ucla.edu.

## 1. Email

**Subject:** Help build an open source lesson with UC OSPO (interest by July 17)

Hi all,

The UC OSPO Network is running four Carpentries Collaborative Lesson Development Training (CLDT) cohorts this summer and fall. The software licensing cohort is full, but three are still forming and we're recruiting. There are two ways in.

**Join an existing team.** The "Librarians as Open Source Stewards" lesson is already scoped. It takes skills librarians use every day, curation, metadata, preservation, community stewardship, and treats them as open source contributions. We need 2-4 people to develop and pilot it.

**Propose your own lesson.** We have two open slots for new lessons that fit the open source and OSPO space: security practices for academic open source, governance models, open source in non-CS research, contributing as a non-coder, reproducibility and open data. To pitch, you just need a topic and a rough sense of who you'd teach. You don't need a team yet, we'll help you build one.

CLDT teams use backward design to turn an idea into a real lesson, and you leave with a full outline and drafted sections. Finished lessons get taught through UC OSPO and partner programs, and can go on to Carpentries Lab peer review, a JOSE or Zenodo DOI, and ongoing maintenance through the network even if you change institutions. One team member needs Carpentries Instructor Training or Learner-Centered Teaching before the cohort starts.

Full details: https://ucospo.net/posts/develop-open-source-lesson-cldt/

Express interest by **July 17**: open an issue at https://github.com/UC-OSPO-Network/education/issues/new?template=cldt-interest.yml or reply to this email. Tell me which option you're after, who'd join you, and for a new lesson, a sentence on the idea.

Thanks,
Tim

## Suggested sequence

1. **Day 0 (send week of June 23):** Email to the per-campus UC OSPO leads asking them to forward, plus post to UC OSPO Slack and CURIOSS #WG-Sharing. This is the warm core.
2. **Day 0-1:** Social posts (LinkedIn + Bluesky/Mastodon). Tag CURIOSS, The Carpentries, US-RSE where you can.
3. **Day 1-2:** Carpentries Slack + Library Carpentry, US-RSE Slack, code4lib (Slack first, then listserv).
4. **Day 2-3:** Email lists (Carpentries Discuss, DLF, UC library lists). Lists move slower, so earlier is better.
5. **~July 7-10:** Short "one week left" reminder on the P1 Slack channels and a social bump.
6. **July 17:** Deadline.
