Tim Dennis directs the Data Science Center at UCLA Library. The center supports researchers across data discovery, management, analysis, publishing, preservation, and reproducible research. He runs UCLA Dataverse, UCLA's institutional data repository, and works on open science and data-sharing across campus.

He led an IMLS-funded project that produced 15 open science lessons for library and research communities. He is UCLA Co-PI for the UC OSPO Network, a Sloan Foundation initiative that coordinates open source program offices across all ten UC campuses. He also coordinates UC Carpentries, which includes more than 60 certified instructors across the system, and co-chairs the Library Carpentry Governance Committee.

He's spent more than 26 years building data services and research infrastructure at UC.
