<h1>UC Open Source as Institutional Infrastructure</h1>
<p>Tim Dennis &middot; Data Science Center, UCLA Library &middot; IASSIST 2026 &middot; Slides + resources: <strong>ucospo.net</strong></p>
From Dissertation to Global Infrastructure 
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">BSD Unix</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Berkeley &middot; 1970s&ndash;80s</div>
  <div class="project-desc">The permissive licensing model that made open source possible. Derivatives (MIT, Apache) now govern billions of lines of production software worldwide.</div>
</div>
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">UCSC Genome Browser</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Santa Cruz &middot; Summer 2000</div>
  <div class="project-desc">PhD student Jim Kent assembled the human genome on 50 borrowed PCs, icing his wrists to keep coding. He finished <strong>three days before</strong> Celera Genomics and its dedicated supercomputer. The genome stayed in the public domain.</div>
</div>
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">Ceph</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Santa Cruz &middot; 2004</div>
  <div class="project-desc">Sage Weil&rsquo;s PhD dissertation on exabyte-scale distributed storage. Merged into the Linux kernel (2010); commercialized through Red Hat acquisition (2014). Proceeds funded <strong>CROSS at UCSC</strong> -the first UC OSPO and the seed of this network.</div>
</div>
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">Apache Spark</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Berkeley AMPLab &middot; 2009</div>
  <div class="project-desc">Matei Zaharia built it so a classmate could beat the Netflix Prize with faster distributed ML. Donated to Apache before commercializing. <strong>Databricks: $60B+.</strong></div>
</div>
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">RISC-V</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Berkeley &middot; 2010</div>
  <div class="project-desc">A three-month summer project to give grad students a chip architecture without paying ARM royalties. Now in <strong>10 billion devices</strong>, adopted by 350+ organizations in 70 countries. Foundation relocated to Switzerland in 2020 to stay neutral in US-China trade tensions.</div>
</div>
<div class="project-accent"></div>
<div class="project-body">
  <div class="project-name">Ray</div>
  <div class="project-origin">UC Berkeley RISELab &middot; 2016</div>
  <div class="project-desc">Two PhD students tired of rewriting Python every time they needed to scale beyond one machine. Now <strong>core OpenAI compute infrastructure</strong>. Donated to the Linux Foundation in 2025.</div>
</div>
