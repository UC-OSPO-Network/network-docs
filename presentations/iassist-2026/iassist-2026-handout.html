From Dissertation to Global Infrastructure
BSD Unix
UC Berkeley · 1970s–80s
The permissive licensing model that made open source possible. Derivatives (MIT, Apache) now govern billions of lines of production software worldwide.
UCSC Genome Browser
UC Santa Cruz · Summer 2000
PhD student Jim Kent assembled the human genome on 50 borrowed PCs, icing his wrists to keep coding. He finished three days before Celera Genomics and its dedicated supercomputer. The genome stayed in the public domain.
Ceph
UC Santa Cruz · 2004
Sage Weil’s PhD dissertation on exabyte-scale distributed storage. Merged into the Linux kernel (2010); commercialized through Red Hat acquisition (2014). Proceeds funded CROSS at UCSC, the first UC OSPO and the seed of this network.
Apache Spark
UC Berkeley AMPLab · 2009
Matei Zaharia built it so a classmate could beat the Netflix Prize with faster distributed ML. Donated to Apache before commercializing. Databricks: $60B+.
RISC-V
UC Berkeley · 2010
A three-month summer project to give grad students a chip architecture without paying ARM royalties. Now in 10 billion devices, adopted by 350+ organizations in 70 countries. Foundation relocated to Switzerland in 2020 to stay neutral in US-China trade tensions.
Ray
UC Berkeley RISELab · 2016
Two PhD students tired of rewriting Python every time they needed to scale beyond one machine. Now core OpenAI compute infrastructure. Donated to the Linux Foundation in 2025.
